ABOUT عن
NAHAWANDI

Bringing culture and context into the club.

Rooted between Amman and Bremen, Nahawandi builds danceable electronic grooves around cultural moments, rhythmic systems, voices and fragments drawn from different worlds—reworking them into high-energy electronic frameworks with unexpected twists.

Derived from the Arabic maqam Nahawand, the name Nahawandi reflects his commitment to bringing new melodic perspectives to contemporary electronic music.
